# Overlap with the original authors

Sources reviewed: the supplied published PDF (Management Science 71(9), 7497–7515), all 11 supplied Stata do-files, and the complete Online Supplement.docx including its tables, from ResearchBox 482 / Zenodo record 15033033. The original files are preserved in the separate reference backup.

**Analysis 1 has substantive overlap.** Study 2.do, section 3.4 “Other Analyses,” lines 219–232, reports condition means and OLS regressions for sb_accurate and sb_trust using the full sample. Lines 259–272 repeat these analyses after attention-check exclusions. A classical one-way ANOVA and their full-sample condition-indicator OLS model test the same underlying group-mean model. This project adds explicit omnibus F tests, Welch sensitivity checks, Holm correction across six unequal-variance pairwise comparisons, simultaneous Bonferroni intervals, and an item-specific confidence-interval figure. It should be described as an extension of the authors’ existing exploratory item analyses, not as a new substantive hypothesis. Supplement 2 also studies accuracy perceptions, but its percentage/error measures (ob_accurate, ob_inaccurate, ob_error) differ from sb_accurate.

**Analysis 2:** no equivalent six-study fixed-effects leave-one-study-out analysis was found in the reviewed main text, complete supplement, or supplied do-files. The supplement does contain fixed effects for app domain in supplementary experiments; those are not study fixed effects or study omissions. The absence claim is limited to these reviewed materials and is not a universal novelty claim.

Both project extensions are exploratory. They are student additional analyses, not estimates attributed to the authors or original preregistered tests.
